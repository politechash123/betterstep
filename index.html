FAQ

Get a Shoe NFT in the BetterStep app and simply walk, run or exercise. Every verified step is converted into $BTT and $BFF rewards that land straight in your in-app wallet.

List your Shoe NFT on the BetterStep marketplace with a fixed price or open it to bids. When a buyer accepts, the NFT is transferred and the payment goes directly to your wallet.

You can refurbish your worn-out shoes. With the refurbishment option, you can repair your shoes or exchange them for a newer model.

Every Shoe NFT is minted with one of four rarity levels: Common, Uncommon, Rare or Legendary. Rarer shoes have a smaller supply and boost your earnings. See the Rarity Level table for details.
